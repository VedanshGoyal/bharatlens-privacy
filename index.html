BharatLens Calc - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

BharatLens Calc ("the app") is a calculator app developed by BharatLens Lab. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The app does not require an account, and it does not request the internet permission or any other sensitive permission (such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage).

Data stored on your device

The app saves your favorites, recent calculators, history, and preferences (such as theme) locally on your device so the app works the way you left it. This data never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not share data with any third party.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Calculation results

Results are provided for general convenience. Please verify important financial, academic, or legal figures independently.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
