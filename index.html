BharatLens Calc - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · App version 1.1.0

BharatLens Calc ("the app") is an all-in-one calculator app for Android (package com.bharatlens.bharatlenscalc), developed by BharatLens Lab. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

BharatLens Calc does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app works fully offline and does not send anything you enter to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

To work the way you left it, the app saves a small amount of information only on your device:

DataWhy
Settings (theme, haptic and click feedback, decimal precision, start-up choice)To remember your preferences
Recently used calculators, how often you open each one, your favorites, and your Home screen order and hidden sectionsTo arrange the Home screen
Standard calculator history (your recent calculations)So you can recall earlier results
A display name, only if you choose to enter oneTo show a greeting on the Home screen
Whether the first-launch introduction has been shownSo it appears only once

Values you type into the other calculators (dates of birth, prices, quantities and so on) are used only to show results and are not saved after you leave the app. None of this information is sent to us or to anyone else. The display name is optional, is never used to identify you, and has no account behind it.

Permissions

  • Internet: the app includes the standard Android internet permission that its framework (React Native / Expo) declares, but it makes no network requests and has no features that need a connection.
  • Audio: the app plays a short key-click sound when feedback is turned on. It does not record audio and does not use the microphone, even though the audio library declares the related Android permissions.
  • Vibration: used for key haptic feedback.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, photos, files, phone number, email or any device identifiers.

Clipboard

When you tap a copy button in a calculator, the result is placed on your device clipboard. The app does not read what is on your clipboard.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it does not share data with any third party. It does not use cookies.

Android backup

BharatLens Calc turns Android backup and device-to-device transfer off for its data. Your settings, history and display name are not copied to Google backup or to a new phone, so after a reinstall or on a new device the app starts empty.

Deleting your data

  • Reset what Home has learned (usage, recents, favorites) or clear history from the app's Settings.
  • Change or remove your display name in Settings.
  • To delete everything the app has stored, clear its storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Calculation results

Results are provided for general convenience. Please verify important financial, academic or legal figures independently.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
